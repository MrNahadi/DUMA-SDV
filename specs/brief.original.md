# Software-Defined Electric Vehicle (SDV) Design Challenge

## Competition Overview

The automotive industry is undergoing one of the biggest technological transformations in history. Modern electric vehicles are no longer defined solely by their batteries and motors—they are increasingly powered by intelligent software. Functions such as battery management, motor control, energy optimization, digital dashboards, diagnostics, connectivity, cybersecurity, and autonomous driving are all coordinated by software.

Today's leading manufacturers are building **Software-Defined Vehicles (SDVs)**, where software determines how the vehicle performs, communicates, and evolves throughout its lifetime. Engineers are therefore expected not only to understand mechanical and electrical systems, but also to design the software architectures that integrate these systems into a complete intelligent vehicle.

The **Software-Defined Electric Vehicle (SDV) Design Challenge** introduces participants to this modern approach to vehicle engineering. Rather than building a physical electric vehicle, teams will design and implement a complete digital EV entirely through software. The challenge focuses on systems engineering, software development, simulation, and digital modelling, allowing participants to experience the complete software development lifecycle of an electric vehicle.

## The Challenge

Imagine you have just been recruited by an electric vehicle manufacturer to join the software engineering team responsible for developing its next-generation Electric Vehicle. Your task is to design the complete software ecosystem that will operate the vehicle.

Instead of manufacturing the car, you will build its digital intelligence. You will define how different vehicle systems communicate, monitor vehicle health, manage energy, interact with the driver, respond to faults, and operate together as one integrated system.

By the end of the competition, your team should present a fully integrated software implementation that demonstrates how a modern electric vehicle would function in the real world.

## Competition Objective

The objective of this challenge is to design, simulate, and implement the software architecture of a modern electric vehicle. Teams are expected to create a complete digital representation of an EV by integrating its major software-controlled systems into one functional platform.

The final solution should demonstrate how software coordinates vehicle operation, monitors performance, manages energy, communicates between electronic modules, and provides an intuitive user experience.

## Deliverables

- **System Architecture Design** – How systems interact to form the vehicle
- **Software Design Documentation** – How the software works
- **Functional Software Prototype** – Simulation software
- **Digital Dashboard** – GUI displaying various information, e.g. vehicle speed
- **Vehicle Simulation** – Behaviour under various conditions, e.g.:
  - Startup
  - Charging
  - Driving
  - System fault notification
  - Regenerative braking
